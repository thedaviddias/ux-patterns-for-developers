## Scope and contact

David Dias operates UX Patterns at uxpatterns.dev and the public connector at
https://mcp.uxpatterns.dev. For privacy requests or support, email
[hello@thedaviddias.com](mailto:hello@thedaviddias.com).
Your AI provider separately controls its prompts, conversations, model processing
and account settings; this policy does not replace that provider's policy.

## Connector requests

The public connector does not require an account or API key. It processes tool
names and arguments to respond, including queries and code snippets submitted
for static review. Submit only information you are authorized to share; avoid
credentials and unnecessary personal or confidential information.

The connector does not edit applications or deploy them. Code checks use local
deterministic heuristics rather than calls to a model provider. Submitted code
is not placed in the public search cache or written to an application database
by the review implementation. Infrastructure or error logs may contain request
context. Network addresses are processed for delivery, security and rate limiting.
Public search responses and query keys may be cached in process memory for up
to five minutes.

## Website measurement and reliability

The production website uses OpenPanel for page views, outgoing-link events and
explicitly tracked attributes. Sentry processes errors and diagnostic context.
Vercel hosts the website and connector and provides infrastructure, logging and
security functions, including BotID protection on newsletter and feedback requests.
These functions may process network addresses, request metadata, browser/device
information, usage events and diagnostic details. This service uses analytics
and monitoring; it does not promise that visitors are never tracked.

## Newsletter and correspondence

Newsletter signup collects your email and submitted subscription metadata, such
as language, product and source domain, where provided. Resend processes
subscriptions and email delivery. To stop newsletter delivery or request changes
to your subscription, email hello@thedaviddias.com. If you contact us, we process
your message and contact details to respond. Do not put private information in
public GitHub issues.

## Purposes and providers

We process information to answer requests, deliver requested newsletters,
provide support, operate and secure the service, diagnose errors and understand
website usage. Information is shared with the hosting, diagnostics, analytics
and email providers described above for those functions. Providers may process
information in countries other than yours under their applicable arrangements.

For information about each provider's processing, see
[Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice),
[OpenPanel's privacy policy](https://openpanel.dev/privacy),
[Sentry's privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/) and
[Resend's privacy policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).
Your AI application's handling of connector calls is governed separately by
that application's terms and privacy settings.

## Retention

The public search cache expires within five minutes. Reviewed snippets are not
retained in that cache or an application database by the review implementation.
Infrastructure logs, diagnostic and analytics records, newsletter subscriptions
and correspondence have separate retention rules determined by their purpose,
provider settings and applicable obligations. Their retention is not the same
as the search-cache lifetime. We do not state a universal deletion period or
promise that no request information can appear in infrastructure logs. Contact
us for information about a particular record or a deletion request.

## Your choices and requests

Email hello@thedaviddias.com to ask about processing or request access,

We may need information to verify your request and locate the relevant records.
Applicable obligations, security needs and provider controls may affect what
can be deleted or supplied. Manage AI-provider conversations and permissions
through that provider. Browser privacy settings may limit some website tracking.
